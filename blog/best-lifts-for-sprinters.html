What Lifts Are Best for Sprinters?

Squat, Olympic lifts, plyometrics, and accessories that transfer to sprint speed—not bodybuilding fluff.

Squats and hinge patterns build the force platform you need off the blocks. Clean and snatch variations train explosive extension if technique is coached well.

Plyometrics should progress from stiffness hops to bounds and depth jumps matched to your training age. Quality reps beat battered ankles from random box jump contests.

Upper body matters for arm drive and posture, but it should not dominate the week. Sprinters are not bench-press specialists.

Integrate lifting with track days—heavy squats the day before a max-velocity session is usually a bad trade.
