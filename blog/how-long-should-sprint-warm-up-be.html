How Long Should a Sprint Warm-Up Be?

Dynamic warm-up structure, activation, and build-ups before max-effort sprint sessions and meets.

Expect 20–35 minutes for a full pre-track session: general movement, mobility, drills, progressive accelerations, then main work.

Meet-day warm-ups may run longer because of check-in, waiting on flights, and re-priming between rounds.

Include ankling, A-skips, wickets, and short build-ups so nervous system and tissues are ready before 95%+ effort.

A rushed warm-up shows up as sluggish blocks and pulled muscles—not mysterious bad luck.
