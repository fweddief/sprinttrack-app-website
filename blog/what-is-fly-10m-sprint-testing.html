What Is a Fly 10m Sprint and Why Test It?

Flying sprints, max velocity testing, and how fly times relate to 100m performance.

A fly 10m (or 30m fly zone) measures max velocity without acceleration confounding the clock. You build in, hit a zone, and time the flying segment.

Improving fly speed while maintaining start quality is how many athletes drop tenths in the 100m.

Test on rested days with full warm-up; compare only when conditions and timing method stay consistent.

Log results next to race results so you see whether the issue is start, top-end, or speed endurance.
