How Many Times per Week Should I Sprint?

Sprint frequency for high school, college, and post-collegiate track athletes without digging a recovery hole.

Most developing sprinters thrive on two to three high-intensity track sessions per week, plus lifting and recovery. Elite groups sometimes add a fourth speed day, but only when sleep, nutrition, and soft-tissue care keep up.

More is not better if quality drops. When fly times slide or hamstrings tighten session after session, volume—not laziness—is usually the problem.

Distributed speed (short accelerations, wickets, hops) can fill gaps between main days without beating up the nervous system.

Track your RPE and completion in a log so you and a coach can see trends—not just one bad day.
