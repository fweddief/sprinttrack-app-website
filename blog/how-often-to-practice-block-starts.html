How Often Should I Practice Block Starts?

Optimal frequency for block starts without burning out your central nervous system.

One to two dedicated start-focused days per week is plenty for most athletes. Extra reps without coaching feedback just ingrain bad angles.

Low-volume, high-attention reps beat twenty full-pressure starts. End on a crisp rep, not a sloppy one.

Pair starts with video when possible—even phone footage in lane two helps.

In competition phases, maintain sharpness with shorter start sessions; don’t rebuild technique from scratch.
