How Do I Improve Max Velocity in Sprinting?

Fly sprints, wickets, mechanics, and strength for top-end speed.

Max velocity requires stiffness, front-side mechanics, and the courage to stay relaxed while moving fast.

Fly 30s, wicket runs, and over-pace assistance (when coached) are staples. Full upright posture too early in development hurts more than it helps.

Strength and plyos support reactivity; extra junk mileage does not.

Film upright phases—overstriding and excessive backside kick are common leaks at max speed.
