When Should Sprinters Wear Spikes vs Trainers?

Spike selection, traction, and when to save calves by training in flats or trainers.

Spikes belong on race day and key speed sessions where traction and stiffness help you rehearse meet conditions.

Much of GPP can live in flats or light trainers to reduce achilles and shin load when volume is higher.

Long-jump spikes, sprint spikes, and middle-distance spikes are not interchangeable—match the plate to the surface (Mondo, asphalt, etc.).

Break in new spikes before championship week to avoid blisters and surprises.
