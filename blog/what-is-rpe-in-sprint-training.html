What Is RPE and Why Should Sprinters Log It?

Rating of perceived exertion for track athletes and how it guides weekly training adjustments.

RPE (rating of perceived exertion) is your session difficulty on a simple scale—often 1–10. It captures fatigue that stopwatches alone miss.

A week of RPE 9 sessions without recovery is a warning sign even if times look fine today. Trends matter.

Coaches use RPE with volume to auto-regulate: hard weeks get support, deloads get triggered when athletes trend flat.

SprintTrack factors recent RPE into plan generation so the next week respects how you actually felt, not just what was on paper.
