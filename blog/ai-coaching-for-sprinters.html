Can AI Help Coach Sprinters?

How AI training plans and video form review complement—not replace—human coaching.

AI excels at structured plan generation from your inputs—PRs, meets, availability—and at scaling feedback when a human coach isn’t on-site.

Video analysis can flag posture and rhythm cues on a schedule you can actually keep (e.g., weekly Film Room reviews).

It does not replace medical care, in-person biomechanical expertise, or teammates who know your history.

Use AI for consistency and periodization; use humans for nuance, emotion, and complex injury decisions.
