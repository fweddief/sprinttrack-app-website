What Should Sprinters Eat During Heavy Training Weeks?

Calories, protein, carbs, and hydration targets for sprint and power athletes across training phases.

Sprinters need enough carbohydrates to fuel glycolytic and alactic sessions, plus protein to recover from lifting and plyometrics. Fat stays moderate; timing matters more than fearing entire food groups.

Hard days need more fuel than rest or mobility days—phase-aware targets beat a single static calorie number all season.

Pre-workout meals should be familiar and easy to digest; post-workout meals prioritize protein and carbs within a few hours of the session.

Hydration supports power output and cramps prevention, especially in hot meet weeks. Log weight in the morning during heavy blocks for a simple dehydration check.
