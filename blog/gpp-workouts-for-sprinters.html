What Belongs in a Sprinter’s GPP Phase?

General physical preparation: volume, general strength, and technical base before SPP.

GPP raises work capacity and durability: tempo, hills, general lifting, mobility, and drill volume at moderate intensity.

You are not trying to peak in GPP—you’re building the foundation so SPP can be sharp without breaking down.

Sprinters still sprint in GPP, but volumes are higher and intensities slightly lower than pre-meet blocks.

Transition to SPP when the calendar says meet-specific work should dominate—not when social media says ‘blocks season.’
