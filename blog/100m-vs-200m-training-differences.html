How Is 100m Training Different From 200m Training?

Compare speed endurance, acceleration emphasis, and weekly structure for short sprints vs the 200m.

The 100m is skewed toward acceleration and max velocity with minimal speed endurance. The 200m adds curve mechanics, speed maintenance, and lactate tolerance in late SPP.

200m specialists need more tempo and special endurance reps at 70–95% race effort, while 100m athletes spend more absolute time on start and fly work.

Lifting overlaps, but 200m runners often carry slightly higher glycolytic conditioning without turning into distance runners.

Personalized plans should tag your primary event so volumes don’t copy marathon templates or pure powerlifter splits.
