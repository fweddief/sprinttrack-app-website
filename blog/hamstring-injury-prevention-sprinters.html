How Can Sprinters Prevent Hamstring Injuries?

Nordics, sprint mechanics, load management, and warm-up habits that reduce hamstring strains.

Hamstring strains often follow spikes in intensity or volume, poor late-race posture, or weak eccentric strength—not just bad luck.

Nordic curls, RDLs, and well-coached sprint mechanics (especially upright positioning and front-side mechanics) belong in a prevention plan.

Never jump from zero sprinting to full competitive volume in one week after a layoff. Build exposure progressively.

If something feels like a tug, not normal soreness, pull back and get assessed. Racing through strains costs entire seasons.
