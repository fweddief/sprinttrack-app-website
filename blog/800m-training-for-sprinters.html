Should 100m/200m Sprinters Train for the 800m?

When speed athletes benefit from aerobic work and when it hurts power output.

Pure 100m specialists rarely need true distance work. Some aerobic capacity helps recovery between rounds and multi-event weekends, but steady jogging won’t fix a slow fly phase.

400m–800m athletes need more structured aerobic and lactate work; don’t copy their mileage if your event is 100m.

Tempo for sprinters is usually submax, short, and purposeful—not jogging miles for its own sake.

Event-specific plans prevent marathon habits from creeping into speed training.
