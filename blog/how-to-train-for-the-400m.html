How Do You Train for the 400m?

Speed reserve, special endurance, lactate work, and strength for the 400m dash.

The 400m is a sprint—but one with a painful metabolic finish. You still need max speed; without it, no amount of grinding tempo saves you.

Special endurance sessions (split 200s, 300s, race modeling) teach pacing without dying at 250m. Tech on the first 100m stays aggressive but controlled.

Strength supports posture in the last 100m. Core and unilateral work reduce hamstring breakdown when fatigue hits.

Deload before championship meets so you carry speed into rounds, not junk fatigue from over-volume lactate days.
