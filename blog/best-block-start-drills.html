What Are the Best Block Start Drills for Sprinters?

Block start drills that improve reaction, projection angle, and first-step power for track sprinters.

Effective block work starts with consistent setup: front knee near 90°, rear knee near 120°, hips loaded but not collapsed. Film your starts—head rising too early and popping upright before step three are among the most common faults.

Use falling starts and medicine-ball throws to feel forward projection without straining upright too soon. Three-point and bullet starts reduce complexity while you groove shin angles and arm action.

Pair technical starts with resisted sprints (sled, band) on low-volume days. The goal is sharp, rhythmic push—not exhausting yourself with twenty full blocks sessions per week.

Weekly video review (even a 5-second clip) helps you catch postural changes before they become habits. SprintTrack’s Film Room is built for that rhythm: one focused breakdown per week with cues and reference imagery.
