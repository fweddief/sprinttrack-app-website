How Should I Taper Before a Big Track Meet?

A practical taper guide for sprinters: volume cuts, intensity maintenance, and rest without losing sharpness.

A taper reduces fatigue while keeping neuromuscular firing high. Cut volume 30–50% in the final 7–10 days before an A-priority meet, but keep some short, fast reps at race-relevant intensity.

Avoid brand-new exercises or heroic lifting tests during taper week. Your nervous system needs familiarity, not novelty.

Sleep and carbs are part of the taper. Arriving under-fueled or sleep-deprived erases well-planned training blocks.

Mark A-meets on your calendar at the start of the season so training software—and your coach—can auto-build toward the right peak.
