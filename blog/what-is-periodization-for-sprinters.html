What Is Periodization for Sprinters?

GPP, SPP, taper, and competition phases explained for 100m–400m athletes and how to peak for A-priority meets.

Periodization is organizing training so fitness and speed peak when it matters—your target meets—not when you happen to feel good in March.

General prep (GPP) builds capacity: volume, general strength, and technical habits. Specific prep (SPP) shifts toward race-pace work, shorter rest, and event-specific demands. Pre-competition and competition phases reduce fatigue and sharpen speed.

Sprinters anchor the macro cycle to A-priority meets on the calendar. B and C meets are tune-ups; your training load should reflect which race actually counts.

Without a calendar-driven plan, athletes often arrive at championships overtrained or under-sharpened. Tools that map phases to meet dates reduce that guesswork.
