Can You Train as a Sprinter Without a Coach?

How self-coached athletes structure weeks, avoid junk volume, and use tools for accountability.

Yes—but you need structure: a calendar, phased goals, and honest logging. Random YouTube workouts won’t peak you for a target meet.

Self-coached sprinters benefit from apps that encode periodization, prescribe paces from PRs, and adapt when life disrupts the week.

Find a training partner or film buddy for accountability on speed days.

Invest in occasional mechanical feedback (clinic, Film Room, or trusted video review) so you’re not guessing on form.
